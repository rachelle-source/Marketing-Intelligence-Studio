# Real-World Workflows

The following workflows represent the primary ways Marketing Intelligence Studio should be used.

Every feature added to the application should improve one or more of these workflows.

---

## Workflow 1 – Research a New Topic

### Goal

Understand what real customers are discussing before creating content.

### Steps

1. Select the client.
2. Open Reddit Research.
3. Search a keyword or competitor.
4. Collect discussions.
5. Extract:
   - Questions
   - Pain points
   - Objections
   - Competitors
   - Common terminology
6. Save research to the Knowledge Base.

### Output

- Reddit discussions
- AI summaries
- Customer pain points
- Content opportunities

---

## Workflow 2 – Create a Blog from Research

### Goal

Turn customer conversations into useful long-form content.

### Steps

1. Select a client.
2. Open saved research.
3. Choose the desired topic.
4. Generate:
   - Outline
   - SEO keywords
   - Blog draft
5. Run Brand Validation.
6. Export to Markdown or Word.

### Output

- Complete blog article
- Meta description
- Suggested title
- Internal linking ideas

---

## Workflow 3 – Generate Social Content

### Goal

Repurpose existing research into social media content.

### Steps

1. Open previous research.
2. Select a topic.
3. Choose a platform:
   - LinkedIn
   - Facebook
   - X
   - Reddit
4. Generate multiple post options.
5. Review and edit.
6. Export.

### Output

- Multiple platform-specific posts
- Hashtag suggestions
- Call-to-action ideas

---

## Workflow 4 – Onboard a New Client

### Goal

Create a complete client profile that every AI feature can use.

### Steps

1. Create a new client.
2. Enter:
   - Company name
   - Website
   - Industry
   - Products
   - Services
   - Competitors
   - Brand voice
   - Target audience
   - Preferred CTAs
3. Save.

### Output

A reusable client profile available throughout the application.

---

## Workflow 5 – Competitive Research

### Goal

Understand how competitors position themselves.

### Steps

1. Select the client.
2. Enter competitor names.
3. Gather:
   - Reddit mentions
   - News
   - Reviews
   - Frequently asked questions
4. Summarize findings.
5. Save insights.

### Output

- Competitive intelligence report
- Messaging opportunities
- Common objections
- Market trends

---

## Workflow 6 – Repurpose Existing Content

### Goal

Generate multiple marketing assets from one source.

### Steps

1. Select a blog or research document.
2. Choose desired outputs.
3. Generate:
   - LinkedIn post
   - Email
   - FAQ
   - Facebook post
   - Landing page copy
4. Validate brand voice.
5. Export.

### Output

Multiple content assets from one source document.

# What We Will Not Build

The purpose of this section is to keep the product focused.

Every feature request should be evaluated against these principles.

---

## We Will Not Build a CRM

Managing leads, opportunities, contacts, and sales pipelines belongs in dedicated CRM platforms.

Marketing Intelligence Studio may integrate with CRMs, but it is not one.

---

## We Will Not Become a Social Media Scheduler

The application helps generate content.

Scheduling and publishing belong in specialized platforms.

---

## We Will Not Replace Professional Design Software

Marketing Intelligence Studio may generate graphics or infographic concepts, but advanced graphic design belongs in tools such as Canva, Adobe Illustrator, and Figma.

---

## We Will Not Become a Project Management Platform

Simple project organization is acceptable.

Complex project management should remain in dedicated software.

---

## We Will Not Auto-Publish AI Content

All generated content must be reviewed by a human before publication.

The user always remains in control.

---

## We Will Not Lock Users into One AI Provider

The architecture should allow additional AI providers in the future.

Claude is the default, but the application should remain flexible.

---

## We Will Not Duplicate Existing Knowledge

Client information should exist in one location only.

Every module should reuse the same data rather than creating copies.

---

# Product Success

Marketing Intelligence Studio succeeds when users can:

✓ Research faster.

✓ Create better content.

✓ Maintain brand consistency.

✓ Reuse previous work.

✓ Support multiple clients from one application.

✓ Spend more time thinking strategically and less time performing repetitive tasks.
