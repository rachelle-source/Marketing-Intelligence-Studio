# KORE Wireless — Terminology

_Source: `kore-content-writer` skill (`references/voice-and-tone.md`, `references/competitive.md`)._

## Preferred Terms

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| IoT connectivity | "wireless" alone (too generic) |
| IoT (in most audiences) | "M2M" (older term — technical-legacy audiences only) |
| "connectivity solutions" **with a qualifier** | "connectivity solutions" unqualified |

## Key Technology Terms

- **eSIM** — embedded SIM; KORE's OmniSIM is GSMA SGP.02-based, spans 500 networks and 195
  countries with automatic network failover.
- **iSIM / vSIM** — emerging SIM form factors; KORE content should acknowledge genuine
  uncertainty about which wins rather than picking a side prematurely.
- **APN vs VPN** — network configuration approaches for IoT connectivity; KORE content gives a
  genuine decision framework, not "it depends."
- **ConnectivityPro** — KORE's platform: SIM lifecycle management, usage monitoring, network
  switching, failover, policy controls, spend alerts, data pooling, webhooks/APIs.
- **CHTS (Connected Health Telemetry Solution)** — KORE's pre-configured, compliance-ready
  vertical solution for connected health.
- **MVNO** — Mobile Virtual Network Operator; typical MVNOs offer simple roaming only. KORE
  positions itself as more than an MVNO (full deployment stack).
- **MNO** — Mobile Network Operator; typically regional-only. KORE positions itself as global.

## Key Verticals (glossary)

- **Connected Health** — remote patient monitoring, clinical trials, cardiac devices, PERS/MPERS
- **Fleet & Logistics** — ELD mandate, GPS tracking, telematics, cold chain
- **Industrial IoT** — manufacturing floor visibility, predictive maintenance, private networks
- **Utilities & Energy** — smart grid, EV charging, water conservation
- **Smart Cities** — wastewater monitoring, smart hydrants, waste management, public safety
- **Retail** — POS connectivity, digital signage, inventory management

## Voice Rules Affecting Word Choice

- Active voice only; avoid buzzwords: "synergy", "disruptive", "revolutionary", "seamless",
  "robust" (without specifics).
- No unsubstantiated superlatives ("the best", "the only") without a proof point.
- Never name competitors by brand name outside an explicitly approved comparison piece.
