## Preferred Terms

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| IoT connectivity | "wireless" alone (too generic) |
| IoT (in most audiences) | "M2M" (older term — technical-legacy audiences only) |
| "connectivity solutions" **with a qualifier** | "connectivity solutions" unqualified |

## Key Technology Terms

- **eSIM** — embedded SIM; KORE's OmniSIM is GSMA SGP.02-based, spans 500 networks and 195
  countries with automatic network failover.
- **iSIM / vSIM** — emerging SIM form factors; KORE content should acknowledge genuine
  uncertainty about which wins rather than picking a side prematurely.
- **APN vs VPN** — network configuration approaches for IoT connectivity; KORE content
  gives a genuine decision framework, not "it depends."
- **SGP.32** — an emerging industry standard; KORE's posture is "here's what's true,
  here's what's overstated," resisting the temptation to overstate what a new standard
  delivers.
- **ConnectivityPro** — KORE's platform: SIM lifecycle management, usage monitoring,
  network switching, failover, policy controls, spend alerts, data pooling, webhooks/APIs.
- **CHTS (Connected Health Telemetry Solution)** — KORE's pre-configured, compliance-ready
  vertical solution for connected health.
- **MVNO** — Mobile Virtual Network Operator; typical MVNOs offer simple roaming only.
  KORE positions itself as more than an MVNO (full deployment stack).
- **MNO** — Mobile Network Operator; typically regional-only. KORE positions itself as
  global.

## Voice Rules Affecting Word Choice

- Active voice only; avoid buzzwords: "synergy", "disruptive", "revolutionary",
  "seamless", "robust" (without specifics).
- No unsubstantiated superlatives ("the best", "the only") without a proof point.
- Never name competitors by brand name outside an explicitly approved comparison piece.
- Brand colors by vertical (for visual/design notes): Connected Health — Navy #004271;
  Fleet & Logistics — Green #3ca461; Industrial IoT — Purple #6d28d9; Asset Tracking —
  KORE Red #ee2939; Communications Services — Charcoal #2b2b2b; Technology/Platforms —
  Blue #2563eb.
