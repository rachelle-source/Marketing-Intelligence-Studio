# KORE Wireless — Products & Services

_Source: `kore-content-hub` skill document (Competitive Positioning & Differentiators
reference), provided directly by user, 2026-08-07 — supersedes the earlier
`kore-content-writer` skill source._

KORE solves the entire IoT deployment stack, not just the SIM layer — this is KORE's
single most important differentiator to communicate.

## ConnectivityPro (Platform)

SIM lifecycle management, usage monitoring and real-time analytics, network switching and
automatic failover, policy controls/spend alerts/usage thresholds, data pooling and cost
governance at scale, webhooks/event streaming/documented APIs.

## Device Sourcing & Validation

30+ hardware partners, device validation in under 15 minutes, device-agnostic — not
locked to a single manufacturer.

## IoT Managed Services (Logistics)

Inventory management, staging/kitting/configuration, forward and reverse logistics,
operated from 4 global logistics hubs.

## Pre-Configured Vertical Solutions

Example: **Connected Health Telemetry Solution (CHTS)** — pre-built, compliance-ready
(HIPAA/GDPR) for remote patient monitoring, clinical trials, cardiac devices, and
PERS/MPERS. Reduces time-to-deployment in regulated verticals.

## eSIM (OmniSIM)

GSMA SGP.02-based multi-IMSI eSIM: 500 networks, 195 countries, automatic network
failover. Production-proven at enterprise scale across millions of active connections —
not a recently launched product still building its track record. Supports eSIM alongside
traditional SIM for maximum flexibility.

## Connectivity Technology

5G, LTE-M, NB-IoT, cellular + LoRaWAN + satellite — multi-technology, not cellular-only.

## Cost Governance at Scale

Data pooling across device fleets, customizable spend alerts and usage thresholds,
policy-driven automation to prevent overages, volume and tiered pricing negotiable for
large deployments, use-case/region/scale-based pricing (not one-size-fits-all).
