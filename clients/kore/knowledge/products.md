# KORE Wireless — Products & Services

_Source: `kore-content-writer` skill (`references/competitive.md`)._

KORE solves the entire IoT deployment stack, not just the SIM layer:

## ConnectivityPro (Platform)

SIM lifecycle management, usage monitoring, network switching, failover, policy controls,
spend alerts, data pooling, webhooks/APIs.

## Device Sourcing & Validation

30+ hardware partners, device validation in under 15 minutes, device-agnostic.

## IoT Managed Services

Inventory management, staging/kitting/configuration, forward and reverse logistics, 4 global
logistics hubs.

## Pre-Configured Vertical Solutions

Example: **Connected Health Telemetry Solution (CHTS)** — pre-built, compliance-ready
(HIPAA/GDPR) for remote patient monitoring, clinical trials, cardiac devices, and PERS/MPERS.

## eSIM (OmniSIM)

GSMA SGP.02-based multi-IMSI eSIM: 500 networks, 195 countries, automatic network failover.
Production-proven at enterprise scale.

## Connectivity Technology

5G, LTE-M, NB-IoT, cellular + LoRaWAN + satellite.

## Cost Governance

Data pooling across device fleets, customizable spend alerts and usage thresholds,
policy-driven automation to prevent overages, volume and tiered pricing for large
deployments.
