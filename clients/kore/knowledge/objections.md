## "Who do I call when something breaks at 2am?"

**Response angle:** KORE isn't just a connectivity vendor — it's an end-to-end IoT
infrastructure partner. From SIM to device to logistics to ongoing lifecycle management,
one point of accountability. Approved line: *"KORE isn't just a connectivity vendor —
it's an end-to-end IoT infrastructure partner."*

## "A vendor that works at small scale will probably work at our scale too."

**Response angle:** *"What works at 5,000 devices in one region rarely scales to 500,000
devices across a multi-vertical global rollout — particularly when governance,
compliance, and cost predictability are on the line."* Frame KORE's cost governance and
logistics as infrastructure maturity, not just a pricing feature.

## "Is KORE just another new/unproven IoT vendor?"

**Response angle:** *"KORE has been managing mission-critical IoT deployments since
2003. That's not a number — it's operational proof."* Cite 6x Gartner Magic Quadrant
recognition and 91+ peer reviews as third-party validation.

## "Does connectivity reliability really matter that much for our use case?"

**Response angle (regulated verticals):** *"When the device is monitoring a cardiac
patient or managing utilities infrastructure, connectivity failure isn't an
inconvenience — it's a liability. KORE is built for that."*

## Competitor-gap framing (use to reposition, not attack)

| If the competitor is... | KORE's answer |
|---|---|
| A simple MVNO (roaming only) | KORE offers a global multi-network architecture |
| A regional MNO | KORE offers localized connectivity + global support in 200+ countries |
| SIM-only | KORE offers an integrated global connectivity ecosystem, not SIM-only |
| New / limited track record | KORE has a multi-decade global track record |
| Cloud-native connectivity platform | Strong developer tooling, but thin on logistics/compliance — KORE covers managed services and regulated vertical depth |

## Hard rules when handling objections in content

- Do not name competitors unless it's an explicitly approved comparison piece.
- Do not claim KORE is "the best" without a specific, substantiated proof point.
- Do not frame competitors as inferior — frame KORE's advantages as right for the right buyer.
- Lead with business outcomes and buyer scenarios, not technology specs.
- Use active, confident assertions ("KORE manages 20M+ active connections globally"), not
  passive language ("KORE is known for...").
