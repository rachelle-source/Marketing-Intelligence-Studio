## Core Promise (Roof)

> "Enterprise IoT connectivity infrastructure — built for real-world deployment at global scale."

Every piece of content should ladder up to this promise.

## Brand Personality & Voice

Professional, informative, authoritative — the goal is to instill confidence by
emphasizing expertise and comprehensive support.

**What KORE sounds like:** confident (20+ years of leadership, without arrogance), clear
(simplifies without dumbing down), action-oriented (leads with outcomes over features),
human and direct (real business problems, not abstract tech), optimistic and
forward-looking (trusted guide through IoT complexity).

**What KORE does NOT sound like:** overly salesy or hype-driven, passive or vague,
buzzword-heavy ("synergy", "disruptive", "revolutionary"), overly futuristic or sci-fi.

**Writing style rules:** active voice; formal but accessible (not casual, not stiff);
lead with business outcomes, follow with technology; reference real use cases and real
industries; avoid unsubstantiated superlatives ("the best", "the only") unless backed by
verifiable data.

## The Four Pillars

| Pillar | Core Message | Key Proof Point | Content Angles |

| **Global** | Localized connectivity and support to grow operations anywhere | 200+ countries and territories | Cross-border deployment challenges; roaming vs. true localized connectivity; global scaling case studies |
| **Scalable** | Connectivity, devices, services, and support to deploy at scale | 4 global logistics hubs, 30+ hardware partners | What "scaling IoT" requires beyond connectivity; hardware/logistics/provisioning; pilot-to-millions deployments |
| **Proven** | Enterprise-grade platforms, processes, and track record | 21M+ active SIMs deployed, founded 2003 | Cost of connectivity failures; track record across verticals; what "enterprise-grade" really means |

## Visual & Imagery Tone (for image recommendations in articles)

Blend modern technology with authentic human storytelling. Moving away from overly
futuristic imagery toward real people, real devices, real-world applications. Good
imagery: specific, clear, product-focused, not too busy. Avoid: sci-fi/fantasy,
meaningless abstract icons, chaotic compositions. Maintain a balance of dark and light
imagery; stylish, contemporary, grounded vibe.
