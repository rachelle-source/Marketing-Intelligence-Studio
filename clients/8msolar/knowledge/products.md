## Warranty Structure (three distinct types — always explain separately)

- **Product warranty** — covers manufacturing defects (analogous to a TV warranty)
- **Performance warranty** — guarantees minimum output over time (e.g. a 400-watt panel
  with a 90% performance guarantee should still produce at least 360 watts after 25
  years — convert percentages to watts to make degradation tangible)
- **Workmanship warranty** — covers the quality of the installation itself

### Solar Payback & Economics

- A 6kW solar system: ~$17,100 upfront, reduced to ~$11,970 after the 30% federal tax
  credit
- Annual generation from a 6kW system: 6,130-10,500 kWh depending on location
- Effective solar electricity rate over 25-year system life: ~$0.05/kWh vs. $0.15/kWh
  national grid average
- Average installed solar system: $3-$4 per watt (2023 national baseline)

### Tesla EV Charging Cost Reference

Based on $0.15/kWh national average and 85% charging efficiency, full-charge costs range
from ~$10.94 (Model 3 Standard) to ~$17.60 (Model S Plaid). With solar power (~5¢/kWh
