# 8MSolar — FAQ

_Source: `8msolar-content-hub` skill document (patterns drawn from the annotated
competitor-comparison examples), provided directly by user, 2026-08-07._

These are the FAQ patterns the brand material demonstrates — real question types already
answered in published competitor-comparison content, useful as a model for new FAQ
sections rather than a single canonical list.

- **"Both hold Chapter 87, so what's the difference?"** (vs. NC Solar Now) — answer with
  the operational difference: 8MSolar has a full-time in-house PE.
- **"Does 8MSolar serve Virginia?"** — yes; 8MSolar serves North Carolina and Virginia.
- **"What does the lifetime warranty actually cover?"** — be specific: product,
  performance, and workmanship warranties are three distinct things (see products.md).
- **"Is 8MSolar more expensive than [competitor]?"** — never dodge; answer directly, then
  reframe toward long-term value (volume purchasing often makes 8MSolar competitively
  priced).
- **"Can the Powerwall 3 power my whole home?"** — practical Tesla product question;
  answer concisely and concretely.
- **"Is the Universal Wall Connector compatible with non-Tesla vehicles?"** — same
  pattern: concise, concrete, searchable.
- **"Who do I call for a warranty claim — the installer or the manufacturer?"** — most
  homeowners don't know this; answering it prevents a frustrating runaround and builds
  trust.

## Rule

FAQ answers should be self-contained (answerable without reading the rest of the
article) — this is what makes them useful for both readers and search snippets.
