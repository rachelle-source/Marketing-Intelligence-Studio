# 8MSolar — Terminology

_Source: `8msolar-content-hub` skill document, provided directly by user, 2026-08-07._

## AP Style Rules

- Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and above
- "%" not "percent" when paired with a numeral (e.g., 10%)
- Abbreviate months with 6+ letters when used with a specific date (Jan., Feb., Aug.)
- No Oxford comma required, but be consistent within an article
- Solar panel types: monocrystalline, polycrystalline, thin-film (lowercase, no hyphens)

## Key Terms

- **Chapter 87 Engineering Firm** — NC license for firms with in-house professional
  engineers; a real credentials differentiator most competitors lack.
- **NABCEP** — North American Board of Certified Energy Practitioners; industry-standard
  installer certification.
- **Net metering** — a program that lets homeowners sell excess solar energy back to the
  grid. Define before using.
- **PowerPair** — Duke Energy's NC solar + battery storage incentive program.
- **TIPS (Tesla Installer Performance Score)** — Tesla's own installer quality metric;
  most homeowners don't know it exists.
- **120% Rule (NEC 705.12)** — the electrical code rule governing how much solar
  generation a home's existing panel can safely accept without an upgrade.
- **Monocrystalline / polycrystalline / thin-film** — the three solar panel technology
  types (see products.md article-anatomy notes for how to introduce each).

## Voice Rules Affecting Word Choice

- No flowery language — see messaging.md's word-choice table.

- Bold key terms/stats sparingly — not every paragraph.
