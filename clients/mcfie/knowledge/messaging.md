## Mission Statement (anchor for all content)

> "Help people keep more of the money they make, grow their wealth, and have financial
> peace of mind."

## The Four Uses of Whole Life (McFie Framework)

The same reasons banks and corporations own life insurance:

1. **Cover liabilities** — death benefit protects against financial loss
2. **Tax-free retirement** — part of a non-qualified retirement plan
3. **Finance asset purchases** — use policy loans to buy income-producing assets
4. **Recover the cost of acquisition** — redirect interest you'd pay others back into your
   own policy

## Perpetual Wealth Code

McFie's branded framework for building sustainable wealth using participating whole life
insurance — an alternative to Bank on Yourself and Infinite Banking Concept programs. Same
underlying asset (whole life), different (independent) approach to design and strategy.

## McFie's Two Criteria

Applied to every client, both must be true:

1. **Is it affordable?** — If you can't pay the premium, you can't do it.
2. **Is it comfortable?** — If it makes you uneasy, you won't stick with it.

## Key Brand Claims (always accurate, never overstated)

- Independent — not affiliated with Bank on Yourself or Infinite Banking Concepts, LLC
- Specializes in *designing* policies for maximum cash value
- Strategy sessions are free, with no obligation to purchase
- Will not sell a policy that won't benefit the client
- Life insurance is an asset — the U.S. Supreme Court agrees
- Can work with any life insurance company to find the best fit

## Brand Personality

Educational, independent, trustworthy, client-first, liberty-minded.
