# McFie Insurance — Products & Services

_Source: `mcfie-content-hub` skill (`references/core-concepts.md`)._

## Core Offering

Independent design and placement of **participating whole life insurance** policies,
engineered for maximum cash value rather than sold off-the-shelf. McFie can work with any
life insurance company to find the best fit for a client, rather than being restricted to
one carrier or marketing-group model.

## Policy Design Criteria

- Mutual (not stock) company
- Participating (dividend-eligible)
- Engineered for maximum cash value: high PUA (paid-up additions) rider, minimized base
- Not a Modified Endowment Contract
- Not bundled with unnecessary term riders
- Target: 60–70% of first-year premium as first-year cash value

## Related Strategy: Perpetual Wealth Code

McFie's branded framework for using a properly designed participating whole life policy for:
covering liabilities, tax-free retirement income, financing asset purchases via policy
loans, and recovering the cost of acquisition.
