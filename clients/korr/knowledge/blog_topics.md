# KORR Medical Technologies — Content Themes

_Source: `KORR_Reddit_Skill.pdf` ("Content Themes to Emphasize" section), provided
directly by user, 2026-08-07. Note: the source material is a personal Reddit-growth
skill, not a company content calendar — these themes were written for community
discussion/comment content, not formal articles, but are the closest thing to a topic
backlog this client has._

1. **VO2 max as a health/longevity marker** — population-level research (all-cause
   mortality), individual variation in response, limitations as a performance predictor
2. **Zone 2 vs. high-intensity tradeoffs** — time commitment differences, adaptation
   mechanisms, personal preference factors
3. **Measuring VO2 max** — lab testing vs. wearable estimates, protocol importance (ramp
   tests, verification stages), why numbers don't match between methods
4. **Training response variability** — genetics vs. training, responders vs.
   non-responders, patience with adaptation timelines
5. **Data interpretation** — HR/power/pace/RPE relationships, when metrics conflict,
   context-dependent meaning
6. **Common mistakes** — intensity-first approaches, ignoring recovery, overvaluing a
   single metric
