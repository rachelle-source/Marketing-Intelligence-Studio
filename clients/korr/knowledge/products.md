## What KORR Makes

Metabolic testing systems used in universities, performance labs, and clinical settings.
Core capability: direct measurement of oxygen consumption via breath-by-breath gas
exchange analysis (indirect calorimetry).

## Key Concepts

1. **Direct measurement vs. estimation** — wearables estimate VO2 max from HR/pace
   models; KORR measures actual oxygen consumption breath-by-breath. This explains why
   lab numbers differ from watch numbers.
2. **Breath-by-breath analysis** — an indirect calorimetry approach; calculates VO2 max,
   metabolic efficiency, and fuel use. Considered the gold standard for accuracy.
3. **VO2 max as capacity, not performance** — represents the aerobic ceiling, not race
   results. Economy, thresholds, and durability also matter; a high VO2 max without good
   economy is unrealized potential.
4. **Health & longevity connection** — a strong predictor of all-cause mortality and a
   cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) marker at the population level, with individual
   variation.
5. **Protocol quality matters** — proper ramp rate, true maximal-effort indicators, and
   verification stages are needed for accuracy; a bad protocol produces misleading
   numbers regardless of the equipment used.

## Who Uses It

Universities, performance labs, and clinical settings — not sold directly as a consumer
product. Individuals typically encounter KORR by being tested at a lab or clinic that
uses the system.
