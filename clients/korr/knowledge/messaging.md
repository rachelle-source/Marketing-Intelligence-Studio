# KORR Medical Technologies — Messaging

_Source: `KORR_Reddit_Skill.pdf` ("Brand Mention Rules" and "Reddit-Safe KORR Framing"
sections), provided directly by user, 2026-08-07._

## Core Factual Positioning

KORR measures actual oxygen consumption breath-by-breath (direct measurement /
indirect calorimetry) — where consumer wearables estimate VO2 max from heart-rate/pace
models. This is the one core distinction all KORR messaging should communicate, stated
factually rather than as a marketing claim.

## Brand Mention Rules (from the source material — apply to any first-person/community content)

**When to mention:**

- Only when contextually relevant to a real story or data point
- Frame as "what I currently use," never as a recommendation
- Mention once, then use pronouns ("it," "the device," "the system")
- Always acknowledge alternatives and tradeoffs

**How to mention (approved pattern):**

> "I got tested at a lab using a KORR system — showed my VO2 max as 52, while my Garmin
> estimated 48."

**Not this:**

> "I highly recommend KORR testing! KORR is the gold standard."

## Absolute Prohibitions

- Never include links unless explicitly asked
- Never repeat the brand name unnecessarily
- Never use praise language ("best," "amazing," "perfect")
- Never make recommendations or coaching suggestions
- Never use the brand name as the subject of a promotional verb

## Tone

Factual, uncertain where genuinely uncertain, non-promotional. This is the opposite of a
typical marketing voice — it's closer to a peer sharing a data point than a company
describing its own product.
