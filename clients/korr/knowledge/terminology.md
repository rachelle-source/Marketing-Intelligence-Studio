# KORR Medical Technologies — Terminology

_Source: `KORR_Reddit_Skill.pdf`, provided directly by user, 2026-08-07._

- **VO2 max** — maximal oxygen uptake; a measure of aerobic capacity.
- **Breath-by-breath analysis** — an indirect calorimetry method that measures oxygen
  consumption and carbon dioxide production on every breath.
- **Indirect calorimetry** — estimating metabolic rate/substrate use from respiratory gas
  exchange, rather than measuring heat production directly.
- **Direct measurement vs. estimation** — KORR measures; consumer wearables (e.g.
  Garmin) estimate from heart-rate/pace models.
- **CRF (cardiorespiratory fitness)** — the broader health marker VO2 max is a component
  of; associated with all-cause mortality at a population level.
- **Ramp test / verification stage** — testing-protocol elements that determine whether a
  VO2 max result is a true maximal effort and therefore accurate.
- **Zone 2 training** — lower-intensity aerobic training; discussed in the source
  material in the context of time-commitment and adaptation tradeoffs vs. high-intensity
  training.
